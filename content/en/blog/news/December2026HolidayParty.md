## Celebrating our Amazing Community of Committers

<strong>Wednesday, December 18th, 2025   8:30-11:00 MT (10:30-1:00 ET) </strong>

<div style="font-size:1.8em;text-align:left;margin-top:10px">

Join us for the Ortelius Open-Source Contributor Holiday Gathering on Friday, December 18, 2026 at 8:30 AM MT. We’ll kick things off with our infamous beer-and-donuts intro (because why choose?) and celebrate our community’s achievements with a special badge recognition for contributors. Come raise a mug, grab a donut, and toast the people who shipped code, docs, and ideas all year long—see you there!


## Agenda 

### 8:30AM MT - 9:00AM MT Beer and Donuts 

Join us for Beer & Donuts, our semi-annual Ortelius tradition celebrating the incredible people behind the project! We'll recognize our committers, award badges, applaud outstanding contributions, and raise a glass (or a donut) to the community that makes Ortelius possible.

### 9:00AM MT - 9:30AM MT Keynote Sleigh Your LinkedIn Game!

<img src="/images/LoriRandle-keynote.png" alt="Lorri Kane Randle" height="300px" width="500px" />

<p></p>

[Lorri Kane Randle](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lorrirandle/) will be our special guest speaker. She will share tips and tricks for growing your LinkedIn following, expanding your professional network, and getting more engagement with your posts. Whether you're a developer, open-source contributor, community leader, or just someone who wants to build a stronger professional presence, this session is for you! We'll talk about how to make meaningful connections, get noticed, and turn your LinkedIn activity into real opportunities—all while celebrating another great year with the Ortelius community.

### 9:30AM MT - 10:00AM MT The Gift of a More Secure Open Source Ecosystem: Introducing Akrites 

<img src="/images/AkritesIntroduction-CFreeman.jpg" alt="Christina Freeman" height="300px" width="500px" /> 
<p></p>
As AI accelerates vulnerability discovery, open source maintainers face increasing pressure to manage security reports and deliver fixes. Join [Christina Freeman](https://www.linkedin.com/in/christina-freeman-4b08062/) for an introduction to Akrites, an initiative focused on collaborative vulnerability management, reducing maintainer burnout, and strengthening open source security. Discover how communities like Ortelius can work together to build a more secure software ecosystem in 2027 and beyond.

#### 10:00AM MT - 11:00AM MT Adoption, Adoption, Adoption, Onboarding Open-Source Projects

<img src="/images/tracyholidayparty.jpg" alt="Tracy Ragan" height="300px" width="500px" /> 

<p></p>

Start working toward your [Pathfinder badge](https://ortelius.io/blog/2026/08/12/become-an-ortelius-pathfinder-put-your-favorite-open-source-project-on-the-map/). [Tracy Ragan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tracy-ragan-oms/) will walk through how to onboard an OS project into Ortelius. In 2027, we want to build adoption for Ortelius. Our pathway, provide post-deployment vulnerability detection services to our fellow Open-Source Projects.
